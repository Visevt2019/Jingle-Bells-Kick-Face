About JBKF

Overview: The JBKF or Jingle Bells Kick Face is a casual, fictional meta‑series built around humor and absurdity. It spans various interconnected arcs, each with its own theme and style, but all united by a playful spirit.

JBKF is interesting, light, and deliberately chaotic — a universe where paradoxes, satire, and the act of kicking faces become part of the lore.

It’s not one genre, but many: a collection of series that together form a humorous, meta‑narrative experiment designed to entertain and surprise.

Characters: Santa, Monica, Ben, Charlie, Paraoh, Diamonde.

Temporary characters: Given above are the main characters of JBKF, all other charecters mentioned in the story are temporary and have less significance.

Additional Info

History: JBKF was originally invented on 8 and 9 December 2022 by Parin. But was officially created on 20 August 2023. V1 in the 'Volumes' series is the main JBKF

Paradox: In JBKF, there is one single paradox that revolves around every series i.e. "Santa kicks face because it written in the poem" and "It's written in the poem because Santa kicks face". Whereas Monica stands in between to fill the gap.
